# A Study on Air Pollution in Bangladesh: Causes, Effects, and Solutions

This research was presented at the **1st International Conference on Social Science (ICSS-2025)**, held at **Begum Rokeya University, Rangpur, Bangladesh**. The study explores the major causes and public health impacts of air pollution in Bangladesh and discusses potential solutions based on public survey findings and secondary sources, aiming to promote environmental awareness and support effective policy development.
